# Tone by country

Starting points, not rules. Adjust to your market and to how well you know the recipient.

| Country | Style | Sign-off |
|---|---|---|
| Poland | Polite, fairly formal, structured, concrete dates, no hype | Kind regards |
| Germany / Austria / Switzerland | Formal, precise, exact dates and owners, no small talk | Kind regards |
| Netherlands / Nordics | Direct, short, plain | Best regards |
| France | Courteous, short polite opening | Best regards |
| UK | Polite, understated, softened asks | Kind regards |
| US / Canada | Warm, outcome-first, short | Best |
| Czech / Slovakia | Factual, modest | Kind regards |
| Ukraine | Brief, direct, peer tone | Best regards |
| Japan | Honorific, indirect, thanks first, no pressure or deadlines on them | With best regards |
| Korea | Respectful, hierarchy-aware | Best regards |
| UAE / Gulf | Courteous, relationship-first, patient | Best regards |
| Australia | Warm, peer-to-peer, casual | Cheers |

Greeting: "Hi [First name]," everywhere, except Japan: "Hi [Surname]-san,".
Formal cultures (Germany, Poland, Japan, Korea) tend to read numbered recaps better than bullets.
